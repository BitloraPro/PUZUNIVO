# Mandatory production 3D content — NOTHING in this document is already installed

The previous project had no imported .fbx/.glb/.gltf character/vehicle/environment models. **Do not substitute primitive cars or pretend CSS backgrounds are cinematic quality.** The Unity scene authoring command fails closed until a manifest references real prefabs.

## Minimum imported, commercial-use-safe content
- **Rider**: detailed rigged female/androgynous skateboarder, consistent proportions and outfits, idle/walk/run/skate/jump/fall/trick clips, Animator controller, foot placement, expression blendshapes.
- **Skateboard**: deck, grip, trucks, four wheels, riding animation anchors.
- **Police**: licensed patrol sedan/SUV (lights, siren, wheels, distinct LODs), helicopter with spinning rotor and chase searchlight, traffic civilian cars.
- **World**: downtown coastal avenue and buildings, roadways/sidewalks, marina docks, palm trees, crossings, overpasses, signage, railings, props, waterfront/ocean.
- **Materials**: 2K/4K source textures (albedo, normal, roughness/metallic, AO) with adaptive mobile/Web compression and quality tiers.
- **Lighting**: authored sunset and atmosphere, reflections, bloom and exposure tuning, mobile baked substitute for costly real-time lighting.
- **Audio**: movement/SFX, sirens, cars, urban ambience, chase music.

Use assets with explicit redistribution/production-license rights. Source archives/licenses and attribution where required must be tracked. Avoid store dependencies that prohibit redistribution of raw assets in a public repo. Source files not yet present are intentional, not permission to present a primitive-greybox as final.

## Acceptance content gates
Hero silhouette readable at 720p Android and 1080p Web; character surfaces no blocky primitive art; moving vehicles are fully modeled, animation state blends cleanly; local asphalt reflections and sunset lighting approximate supplied reference; stable mobile frame budget and PS5 build quality reviewed by a console-authorized engineer.
